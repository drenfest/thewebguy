### RR-003 — confirm the white-label development offer

The page is credible for agency-side WordPress support, maintenance, fixes, SEO implementation, and QA. The query's leading results describe complete website builds, custom themes and blocks, WooCommerce work, migrations, and an explicit delivery process. The smallest action is to confirm whether those capabilities are genuinely offered. If they are, a later approved change can add one precise delivery-scope section with real examples and boundaries. If they are not, keep the page positioned around support and stop treating the development query as a content target. This helps agencies choose the right service even if rankings do not improve. Keep the current title, meta description, white-label boundaries, and support positioning unchanged. Evidence limitation: the September month is incomplete and no sales-capability record confirms full-build scope.

### RR-005 — move the existing repair handoff earlier

The article satisfies triage intent thoroughly, but its hands-on service path appears well after the initial diagnostic material while the query and current results skew commercial. Reuse the existing Website Fixes/repair handoff as a compact decision block immediately after the quick diagnostic checklist; do not add speed, price, guarantee, or availability claims. This helps readers decide between safe self-triage and paid repair sooner. Keep the H1, body depth, parent/child role, title, and meta description unchanged. Evidence limitation: the accessible result set is not controlled for country, device, or personalization.

### RR-008 — resolve the Janesville offer boundary before expanding

The page offers technical SEO implementation and website support. Current Janesville results prominently offer Google Business Profile work, listings, map visibility, ads, content, and locally based proof. Confirm which local-SEO deliverables and service-area claims are real before changing copy. If the broader offer is not available, keep the page as technical website support and monitor. This prevents a misleading agency-style promise. Keep the current metadata, H1, remote/local-friendly framing, and technical proof unchanged. Evidence limitation: map-pack and controlled Janesville-location results were unavailable.

### RR-010 — investigate Freeport hub/child overlap

The exact query appeared for both `/locations/freeport-il/` (16 impressions, position 15.8) and `/locations/` (6 impressions, position 15.5). The hub itself uses the title and H1 `Local Website Support Near Freeport, IL`, so the split has a plausible structural cause. Review the wider Freeport query family and intended hub role before deciding whether to reduce Freeport-specific targeting on the hub or strengthen the hub-to-child distinction. This helps users land on the most specific page. Keep both pages unchanged until that investigation is approved and completed. The evidence supports an overlap investigation, not a cannibalization conclusion.
