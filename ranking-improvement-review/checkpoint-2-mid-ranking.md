## Proposed investigations

### RR-013 — confirm the emergency operating offer

The page accurately covers urgent WordPress symptoms and uses an exact title and H1. The result set, however, strongly emphasizes operating commitments such as 24/7 or on-call availability, rapid response, phone or live-chat intake, priority queues, published pricing, and guarantees. Confirm actual hours, intake, prioritization, response expectations, pricing, and emergency qualification before changing copy. Metadata must not invent an operating promise. Keep the current title, description, H1, symptom coverage, and related routes unchanged.

### RR-015 — define the WooCommerce support model

The page already describes technical WooCommerce help for checkout, products, plugins, tracking, schema, data, and performance. Current commercial results commonly package those tasks with maintenance plans, updates, security, store builds, 24/7 assistance, or a formal ticket process. Confirm whether the real offer is one-off technical help, recurring maintenance, store development, or a combination before expanding the page. The smallest next step is an offer decision, not a metadata rewrite. Keep the current metadata and service scope unchanged until that decision is made.

- RR-019: the unusual local query has only 22 September impressions and no competing site URL. The location page is broader than debugging by design; do not retarget it on this sample.
- RR-020: “WordPress fixer” is ambiguous and low-volume. The page already offers scoped fixes for common WordPress symptoms and improved from 32.9 to 27.0.

## Result-pattern evidence

- White-label support results emphasize invisible agency delivery, maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, ticket handling, and communication processes. The current page matches that support intent.
- Structured-data results mix implementation guides with professional implementation services; the current skills page serves the hired-help subset without overstating outcomes.
- Emergency-support results emphasize concrete availability and response commitments. The gap is operational, not a missing keyword.
- WooCommerce-support results include the official support policy, expert directories, and providers bundling maintenance, fixes, updates, builds, and support channels.
- “WordPress plugin developer” results mix the official [Plugin Handbook](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/) with hiring and service pages. “JavaScript issues” results skew toward technical diagnosis such as the [WordPress browser-debugging guide](https://developer.wordpress.org/advanced-administration/debug/debug-javascript/) and [Google's JavaScript troubleshooting guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/fix-search-javascript).
- WordPress troubleshooting and fixer results span diagnosis-first services, fast-fix offers, forums, and guides. No recommendation relies on an unverified speed, price, or guarantee claim.

## Evidence basis and limitations

- Primary performance source: the local page/query dashboard and `search-console-page-query-comparisons-2026-10-01.csv`.
- September covers 2026-09-01 through 2026-09-29 and is incomplete. The latest complete week is 2026-09-21 through 2026-09-27.
- Exact page/query rows were kept separate from page totals. August and weekly comparisons are shown only when the exact pair exists.
- All ten local rendered pages returned the expected self-referencing canonical and page-specific title/H1. No full-site crawl was repeated.
- Current exact-query searches were inspected on 2026-10-01. Country, device, precise locality, ads, map packs, login state, and personalization were not controlled.
- The selected sample is diagnostic, not exhaustive. Zero clicks and small impression counts do not by themselves prove a snippet or page defect.
