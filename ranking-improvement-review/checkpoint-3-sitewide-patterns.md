## Recurring monitor-only patterns

### Mixed intent is not a site-template defect

RR-001, RR-004, RR-009, RR-016, RR-017, RR-018, and RR-020 sit in result sets that mix guides, official documentation, tools, forums, marketplaces, and service pages. Their current pages already serve a credible subset of that intent. A sitewide content-length, FAQ, schema, or keyword-density change is not justified. Keep page purpose clear and monitor the exact query families.

### Recent or improving relevance needs time

RR-002, RR-006, RR-007, RR-011, RR-012, and RR-014 either improved materially, reached stronger weekly positions, or entered a recent observation window. RR-001 and RR-005 also have protected metadata tests. Avoid concurrent changes that would make those tests harder to interpret. RR-005's approved content-organization direction remains deferred through 2026-10-30.

### Local-commercial SERPs expose a bounded offer

Janesville and Freeport results favor agencies with broader local-marketing packages and stronger locality signals; Rockford is a smaller supporting example. The confirmed offer is local-friendly technical website support and website-side SEO implementation, not off-site optimization, backlink work, a full local-marketing retainer, or a physical office in every city. Keep that boundary explicit rather than broadening the location template.
