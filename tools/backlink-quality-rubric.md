# Backlink / Citation Quality Rubric

Use this before submitting `https://thewebguy.app` anywhere.

## Greenlight Criteria

Score a target from 0-100. Submit only when the score is 70+ unless there is a clear strategic reason.

- Relevance, 0-25: local business, web design, web development, WordPress, SEO, ecommerce, software, startup, or founder communities.
- Trust, 0-25: known platform, real audience, moderated listings, recognizable brand, or clear editorial standards.
- Profile value, 0-20: supports business name, NAP, website URL, service categories, description, logo, portfolio, hours, and owner control.
- Link quality, 0-15: crawlable public profile or citation page; no forced reciprocal link, badges, paid link scheme, or keyword-stuffed anchor.
- Risk control, 0-15: no auto-approve spam footprint, no adult/gambling/crypto neighborhoods, no mass-submission network, no required backlink.

## Automatic Rejects

- Requires a reciprocal link or badge to publish.
- Sells dofollow links as the main product.
- Auto-approves thin profile pages with no moderation or real users.
- Has unrelated categories only.
- Hides ownership, contact details, or terms.
- Looks like spun directory content, expired-domain reuse, or a backlink farm.
- Requires publishing fake reviews, fake clients, or fake office locations.

## Status Values

- `greenlight`: good enough to submit.
- `watch`: maybe useful, but needs manual inspection first.
- `blocked`: good target but blocked by verification, CAPTCHA, domain email, payment, or owner login.
- `submitted`: submitted but awaiting confirmation/review.
- `verified`: live and owner-controlled.
- `rejected`: reviewed and intentionally skipped.

## Practical Rule

Do not chase 300 links. Chase 300 candidates and keep only the ones that deserve to be attached to the domain.
